IDAS-R

Internal Dialogical Activity Scale – Revised (IDAS-R)  
**INSTRUCTION:** The following statements relate to your thinking about yourself and others. Read each of them carefully. Answer by circling one of the numbers that best describes your way of thinking. Please take your time and think carefully about each item.

**Scale:** 1 \= Never | 2 \= Seldom | 3 \= Sometimes | 4 \= Often | 5 \= Very Often

---

1. I ask myself questions and try to answer them.  
2. My inner dialogues with myself and others hinder me from focusing on what I have to do.  
3. I imagine fictitious scenarios of conversations and events.  
4. When I remember the words that others have spoken to me in the past, I respond to them in my mind.  
5. I play out my internal dilemmas as discussions going on in my thoughts.  
6. In my mind, I discuss past thoughts I've had.  
7. I like to talk with the better side of my personality.  
8. When I don't know something and I cannot ask anybody about it, I try to figure it out in my thoughts.  
9. It is easier for me to make a decision if I first carry out a discussion about it in my thoughts.  
10. Imagining conversations feels strange to me.  
11. Before an important meeting I visualize scenarios of discussions, imagining who will say what.  
12. I "hear" the words that were spoken to me in the past as if they are directed to me now.  
13. When I cannot find a definite solution for a conflict, I talk to myself.  
14. I discuss with myself how my failures could have been avoided.  
15. My "good" side argues with my "bad" side.  
16. When talking to myself, I experience very strong conflicts that prevent me from finding a clear solution.  
17. Thanks to dialogues with myself, I understand myself better.  
18. It is very unpleasant to argue with myself in my thoughts.  
19. In my thoughts, I debate the arguments of someone I am disagreeing with.  
20. I carry on discussions in my mind with the important people in my life.  
21. I talk with myself about those things that are important to me.  
22. Some of the inner dialogues I have with myself and others heighten my sense of misfortune.  
23. My internal conversations make it difficult for me to feel whole.  
24. I discuss my problems with myself as if they are someone else's problems rather than my own.  
25. Through internal discussions, I come to certain truths about my life and myself.  
26. The conversations in my mind upset me.  
27. I continue past conversations with other people in my mind.  
28. When I am alone, I catch myself conversing with someone in my thoughts.  
29. I talk to myself.  
30. I have conversations in my mind which confuse me.  
31. I argue with that part of myself that I do not like.  
32. In my thoughts I take the perspective of someone else.  
33. Thanks to dialogues with myself, I can answer the question, "Who am I?"  
34. I would prefer not to carry on internal conversations.  
35. When preparing for a conversation with someone, I practice the conversation in my thoughts.  
36. When I cannot speak with someone in person, I carry on a conversation with him/her in my mind.  
37. I converse with myself.  
38. After failures, I blame myself in my thoughts.  
39. I feel that I am two different people, who argue with each other, each wanting something different.  
40. When I have a difficult choice, I talk the decision over with myself from different points of view.

---

**Scoring:** A total score is obtained by summing all 40 items. Subscale scores are computed by summing the five items belonging to each subscale:
